01 — BRAND FOUNDATION

Positioning

Magic Picks stocks and ships from a Mt Wellington warehouse — no 30-day dropship wait. Founder Jing personally tests every product before it goes up for sale. Practical quality at fair prices: not luxury, not clearance, not "as seen on TV."

95% of traffic arrives from paid social video. The site is a landing page that catches ad clicks, not a catalogue to browse — one SKU gets the spotlight. Every decision here is built to survive a 3-second scroll on a phone.

DIFFERENTIATORS
→ Auckland local stock, ships within 24 hours
→ Curated by Jing, tested before it's stocked
→ 14-day returns, 12-month warranty
→ A real Auckland person answers the email
NOT BUILT FOR
Gamers, teens, luxury shoppers, "aesthetic Instagram" browsing.
DISTINCT FROM MAGIC ENGINE (PARENT B2B BRAND)

No Golden Ochre, no Space Grotesk, no chip logomark. A customer should never sense this is the same company as our B2B agency.

DISTINCT FROM NZ COMPETITORS

Not Sihoo's red/black gaming edge. Not Ecosa's sage-and-cream wellness minimalism. Not The Warehouse or Kmart's mass-market red. Warm, curated, honest — a little bit personal.
