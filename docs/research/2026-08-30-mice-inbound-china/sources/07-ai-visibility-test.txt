Q: Can US citizens enter China visa-free to attend a business conference or trade exhibition?
================================================================================
**Generally, no.** U.S. citizens are **not** included in China’s general 30-day visa-free entry list, so they normally need a visa to enter China for a business conference or trade exhibition.[1][8]

There are a few narrow exceptions:

- **Visa-free transit** can apply if the trip is only a stopover to a **third country/region**, but that is for transit, not for attending a conference or exhibition as the main purpose of travel.[3][11]
- China’s visa-free policy **does** cover entry for **conferences and exhibitions**, but only for nationals of the specific countries on the eligible list; the United States is not on that list.[1]
- The U.S. State Department also states that **Americans need visas to visit China**.[8]

So, if a U.S. citizen is going to China **primarily to attend a business conference or trade exhibition**, they should assume they **need a Chinese visa**.[1][8]
