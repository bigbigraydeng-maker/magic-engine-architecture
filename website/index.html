Three expansion paths

Different starting points. The same next-market problem.

A new market changes the customer, the trust signals and the channel mix. We help businesses make that change deliberately—not by translating an old playbook.

01 · China to Global

Build trust in an English-speaking market.

For China-based brands entering their first or next overseas market and needing a locally credible route to demand.

02 · Diaspora to Mainstream

Grow beyond the community that launched you.

For diaspora-founded businesses ready to reach the broader local market without losing their original advantage.

03 · Market to Market

Turn one proven market into the next expansion playbook.

For brands already established in one overseas market and preparing to enter another with tighter control.

What you receive

A clear scope. Work you can review.

Start with the problem affecting your business. Your proposal specifies the deliverables, responsibilities, timing and price.

01 · REVIEW

A market and channel review

A summary of the customer, competitors and current marketing, with sources and the most useful next actions.

02 · DELIVERY

Agreed changes and campaign materials

Depending on your scope: localised copy, website improvements, social content or advertising materials. You review the work before publication.

03 · FOLLOW-UP

A results review and next steps

Compare agreed measures such as enquiries, cost per lead or search traffic over a stated period. We explain data gaps and recommend what to continue or change.

Our working method: IMPACT

Inspect → Measure → Prescribe → Act → Check → Tune. Review the evidence, set a baseline, choose an action, deliver the agreed work, check results and adjust the next decision.

AI assists research, drafting and analysis. People remain responsible for business judgement and approvals. Available tools and workflows are confirmed for each engagement.

Good to know

What Magic Engine is—and is not.

Is Magic Engine software or a consulting service?

You can discuss a managed project with us now. We agree the work and price before starting. The self-service platform is opening separately in stages; joining its waitlist does not start a paid engagement.

Which markets do you support?

Our strongest operating context currently connects China, Australia and New Zealand. We assess other markets case by case based on the customer, category, channel access and evidence available.

Do you market overseas products or services into China?

No. Our focus is China-based brands entering overseas markets, diaspora-founded businesses reaching the local mainstream, and proven brands moving from one overseas market to another.
